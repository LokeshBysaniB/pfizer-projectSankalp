Overview

What this tool can do

Two policy checks for Pfizer GM trade channel. Answers come from prepared data and coded rules — recommendations only; people decide.

Capabilities

Scheme cap

Free goods at PTS must stay within 3% of a quarter's primary sales (CO-POL-014 §4.4).

  • See how a past or current quarter performed against the cap
  • Estimate a planned offer (ratio or extra free goods) before a circular goes out
  • Total excess across quarters, for Controller and CFO review

Claims window

Near-expiry and expired claims must fall in the 90-day windows before or after batch expiry (claims policy §5.2).

  • Check one new claim at intake (batch, dates, outcome)
  • Summarise credited claims outside the window and amounts
  • Download the full register for recovery work (firm, confirm, cannot_verify)
